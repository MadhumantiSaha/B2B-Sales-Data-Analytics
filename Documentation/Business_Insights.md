**Insight 1** : West region is the strongest performer in absolute Net Sales, but it is not the most efficient. Other regions (especially North) show better Profit Margin %, indicating that volume alone is not driving profitability.			
**Insight 2** : Target Achievement is uneven across regions. While some regions are close to or above target, South and East are consistently lagging. This suggests the need for region-specific sales strategies or target recalibration.			
**Insight 3** : Returns are a hidden drag on performance. The overall Return Rate is notable, with “Quality Issue” and “Damaged” being the most frequent return reasons. Orders with higher Delivery Days also tend to have more returns and lower customer ratings.			
**Insight 4** : Software category generates the highest Profit, while Furniture and some Services show thinner margins.			
**Insight 5** : Mid-Market/Enterprise customers + better channel mix can lift both sales and margins.
